This is an updated, interactive version of "The Spike Lee Heat Index" originally published by Slate in August 2012 by Browbeat. The original concept and temperatures for the 2012 films are borrowed from that piece. Temperatures for newer films are estimated based on their settings and on-screen climate. All Rotten Tomatoes scores have been updated to reflect current ratings.
